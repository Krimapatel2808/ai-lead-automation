# LeadFlow — AI Revenue Recovery for E-commerce

LeadFlow is an AI-assisted revenue recovery system designed for e-commerce and D2C brands.

It identifies high-intent shoppers, detects the main barrier preventing purchase, and recommends the next best action to help recover potential sales.



## 🚀 What Problem Does LeadFlow Solve?

E-commerce businesses receive large amounts of customer activity across their website, carts, checkouts, chats, and other channels.

The problem is not simply getting more visitors.

The problem is identifying:

- Which shoppers are most likely to buy?
- Who is close to purchasing but hasn't converted?
- What is preventing the purchase?
- Should the business follow up, answer a question, resolve delivery concerns, or offer a discount?
- Which opportunities deserve attention first?

LeadFlow turns these signals into prioritized revenue-recovery opportunities.
